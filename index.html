About us

Care that starts with a calm room.

Dr Umar Younas Dental Clinic is located inside Noor General Hospital in Islamabad. Dr Umar is a Dental Surgeon (B.D.S, R.D.S) who treats patients with implants, orthodontics and general dental problems.

Every visit begins with listening. You get a clear explanation of what is wrong, what the options are, and what each one involves, before any treatment starts.

  • Clean, hygienic treatment. Fresh gloves and covered instrument trays at the chair.
  • Modern dental units. Overhead lighting and adjustable chairs for comfortable treatment.
  • Inside a hospital. Noor General Hospital runs a 24-hour emergency service in the same building.
